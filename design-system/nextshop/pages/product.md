# Product page

Operate. The shopper decides and adds the product to the cart.

- Photograph on the start side, unframed as a card. Purchase facts stay in a hairline rail that sticks on desktop.
- Description and reviews sit under the photograph as sections. They are not tabs.
- One sale signal: struck compare price, and a percent only when the catalog computed one. No badge row, no icon trust chips, no fabricated shipping claims.
- Variant swatches are at least 44px. Stock is a word. The buy control is one full-width button.
- Empty gallery is a quiet field with a label. Mobile order is photo, then the buy rail, then the story.
