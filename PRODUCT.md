# PRODUCT.md

## Product
ShopCMS multi-tenant storefront. The **minimal** theme is a fashion/decor commerce surface for Persian RTL shops.

## Audience
Shoppers browsing collections and products on mobile and desktop. They want calm clarity, fast scanning, and low-friction add-to-cart.

## Brand / product lane
Editorial commerce — quiet, precise, merchandise-first. Visual authority is the Ecomus fashion/decor sample in `_temp/minimal-sample` (hairline borders, monochrome + sale red, portrait product cards).

## Voice
Direct Persian. Short. No hype adjectives. Controls name the action.

## Anti-references
Purple gradients, Inter-as-display, cream+terracotta clusters, glassmorphism decoration, emoji icons, eyebrow/kicker labels above headings, nested card stacks.

## Platform
Web storefront (Django templates + CSS). RTL (`dir=rtl`), Iranian fonts (IRANYekan).
