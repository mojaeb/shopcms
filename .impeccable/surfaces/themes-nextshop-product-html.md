# Product

Operate. Shoppers on mobile and desktop need to see the merchandise, choose a variant, and add it to the cart.

## Direction
Configuration sheet (seed af3d6137, re-roll 1, candidate 4): a data sheet you can order from. Photo plate beside a ruled order sheet where every option is a row and the price is a live line. Below it, a section index, a spec table built from real product data, reviews, and related items. Phones get a bottom buy bar. Tenant accent only on the buy action and the active option; 4px corners and hairlines, no shadows on the page body.

## Constraints
Do not invent trust claims, shipping promises, or badge rows. Description and reviews stay visible sections. Variant, cart, wishlist, and comment behavior stay as they are.
