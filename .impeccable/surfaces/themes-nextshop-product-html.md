# Product

Operate. Shoppers on mobile and desktop need to see the merchandise, choose a variant, and add it to the cart.

## Direction
Sticky purchase rail inside the existing NextShop world: paper ground, IranYekan, teal reserved for the buy button and links. The photograph and the written story scroll; the name, price, variants, quantity, and one buy button stay in a hairline column.

## Constraints
Do not invent trust claims, shipping promises, or badge rows. Description and reviews stay visible sections. Variant, cart, wishlist, and comment behavior stay as they are.
